Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Theo Ebook is a private tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive to list, download and upload the owner's own e-book files and a small catalogue, so they can be synced between the owner's computer and e-book reader.

Data accessed

Files and folders in the owner's Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, with the owner's explicit consent.

How data is used and stored

Drive data is used only to show and transfer the owner's books. Files are stored only on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google Drive. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and reader.

Sharing

No data is collected by, sent to, sold to or shared with anyone else. There are no analytics, ads or third-party services.

Theo Ebook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
